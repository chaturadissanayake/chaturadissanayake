# Security Policy

This policy covers repositories under [@chaturadissanayake](https://github.com/chaturadissanayake) that do not have their own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in one of my projects, please report it privately rather than opening a public issue.

- Email: [consultchatura@gmail.com](mailto:consultchatura@gmail.com)
- Subject line: `Security: <repo name>`

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the potential impact if known. I'll acknowledge reports within a few days.

## What to expect

These are independent and personal projects, not commercially supported software. I'll investigate and fix confirmed issues on a best-effort basis, and credit you in the fix if you'd like. There is no bug bounty.

## Scope

Applies to code in my public repositories. It does not cover third-party services, dependencies, or hosting platforms I use, those should be reported to their own maintainers.
