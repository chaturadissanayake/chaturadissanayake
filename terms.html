Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 13, 2026

These terms apply when you hire me for freelance design or communications work, whether that's through Fiverr, direct contact, or any other channel. I want them to be clear and fair, not a wall of legalese. If anything here is unclear, ask me before we start — that's always easier than sorting it out afterward.

Scope of work

Every project starts with an agreed scope: what I'm delivering, in what format, and by when. This is usually confirmed in writing, whether that's a Fiverr order description, an email, or a short brief we agree on together. Work outside that scope — a new deliverable, a different format, an added platform — is treated as a new request and may involve additional time or cost, which I'll always flag before starting.

Revisions

Most packages include a set number of revision rounds, agreed upfront. Revisions cover refinements to the agreed brief: adjusting colours, copy, layout, or similar. They don't cover a full change of direction partway through — if the brief changes significantly after work has started, that's treated as a new scope and quoted separately.

Payment

For work booked through Fiverr, payment is handled entirely through Fiverr's own system, and their terms apply to that transaction. For direct client work, I typically ask for an upfront deposit before starting, with the remainder due on delivery or per an agreed schedule for larger projects. Final files are released once payment is complete.

Timelines

Delivery dates are estimates based on the agreed scope and my current workload, and I'll flag as early as possible if something's going to run late. Delays caused by slow feedback, late file delivery, or scope changes on the client's side aren't counted against the original timeline.

Ownership & usage rights

Once a project is paid in full, the client owns the final delivered files and can use them as agreed for the project. I retain the right to showcase the work in my portfolio, on this site, and on social media, unless we've specifically agreed otherwise in writing — for example, for a project under NDA or embargo.

Any source files, working drafts, templates, or design systems I use to produce the final work remain mine unless their transfer was specifically agreed and paid for as part of the project.

Third-party assets

Where a project uses licensed assets — stock photography, fonts, icons, or similar — those come with whatever license terms the original provider sets. I'll let you know if a project depends on a license that needs to be renewed or doesn't transfer with the final files.

Cancellations

If a project is cancelled after work has started, payment is due for the work completed up to that point. Any deposit already paid is non-refundable once work has begun, since it reserves time set aside for the project.

Confidentiality

I treat client information, unreleased materials, and project details as confidential by default, and I'm happy to sign an NDA if a project requires one. I won't share draft work publicly before it's approved for release.

Limitation of liability

I do good-faith, professional work, but I can't guarantee specific business outcomes — sales, engagement, or otherwise — since those depend on many factors outside a designer's control. My liability for any project is limited to the amount paid for that project.

Communication

Most projects are managed through Fiverr's messaging system or direct email. I aim to respond within 1–2 business days. If a project needs faster turnaround on communication, let me know upfront and we can agree on that as part of the scope.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, I'll update the date at the top. For work already agreed and in progress, the terms in place at the time we started apply.

Questions

If anything here needs clarifying before, during, or after a project, email me directly at consultchatura@gmail.com.
